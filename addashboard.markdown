**Ad Dashboard** is an unofficial Android app that shows the estimated earnings and ad metrics of your own AdMob account.

*   Today, yesterday, last 7 days, this month and last month at a glance, with changes against the previous period
*   Breakdowns by app, ad unit, country and ad format (earnings, impressions, eCPM, match rate, CTR) and daily charts
*   Home screen widgets, and CSV / PDF export of your reports

**How your data is handled.** You connect your Google account with read-only access to AdMob. The app reads your reports directly from the AdMob API on your device. There is no server: your AdMob data is never sent to the developer, and it is stored only on your device. You can disconnect at any time in the app, which revokes the app's access and deletes the stored data.
